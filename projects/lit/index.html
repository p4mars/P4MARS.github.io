Luchtvaart in Transitie (LiT)

Luchtvaart in Transitie is a Dutch national programme that brings together government, knowledge institutes and industry to accelerate the transition to climate-neutral aviation by 2050. Within LiT, p4MARS contributes perception and AI for the production and maintenance of next-generation aircraft through two activities: fine-detailed 3D modeling for monitoring manufacturing processes, and automated non-destructive testing.

Sponsored by Nationaal Groeifonds National Growth Fund

Activities

Fine-Detailed 3D Modeling for Monitoring Manufacturing Processes

Advanced manufacturing needs to see what is happening to a part while it is being made. We develop high-fidelity 3D and 4D reconstruction methods that capture fine geometric detail and its evolution over time, turning sensor streams into evolving digital twins of the process. Applications include in-process inspection and quality control, and the manufacturing and recycling of composite structures.

Automated Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Inspecting aircraft structures for hidden damage is slow, manual work. We develop robotic, perception-driven non-destructive testing that plans and executes inspections autonomously, registers sensor data to a digital model of the part, and flags defects for maintenance.
