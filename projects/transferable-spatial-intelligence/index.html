Transferable Spatial Intelligence

Robots that understand a 3D scene not only as geometry, but as objects, their functional parts, and what can be done with them, and that carry this understanding across environments, tasks and embodiments.

We combine 3D scene graphs with vision-language foundation models to obtain open-vocabulary, task-driven perception that transfers to new settings without retraining. The goal is actionable perception: given a task in natural language, a robot should know where in the scene to look and where to act.

T-FunS3D: Task-Driven Hierarchical Open-Vocabulary 3D Functionality Segmentation

T-FunS3D segments the functional parts of a 3D scene (handles, knobs, buttons, drawers) directly from a natural-language task description. It builds an open-vocabulary 3D scene graph from posed RGB-D frames and reasons hierarchically from the scene to objects to their functional parts, so a robot is told not just what is in the room but where to act to complete a task. Presented at the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2026 in Vienna.

Demo · Robots in Business 2026 · RoboHouse, Delft · 19 Nov 2026

From Language to Action with Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Understanding

At Robots in Business 2026, the annual RoboHouse event on business applications of robotics and embodied AI, we demonstrate open-vocabulary navigation and manipulation built on T-FunS3D: a robot receives a task in plain language, finds the relevant object and functional part in the scene, and acts on it. The demo shows how transferable spatial intelligence moves from a research result to a working robot.
