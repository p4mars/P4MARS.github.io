Research pillars

Spatial & Agentic Intelligence

Geometric and semantic scene understanding, 3D scene graphs, and open-vocabulary perception that let robots reason about where things are and what they can do with them.

Related projects Transferable Spatial Intelligence Open-Source Interactive Education Materials for ML and Foundation Models Modeling and Simulation of Disruption for Resilient Infrastructures Foundation Models for SLAM Swarm Localization for Planetary Rovers

Actionable World Models

Interaction-aware models of the physical world that couple perception with action, supporting active and distributed perception for embodied, physically grounded agents.

Related projects Transferable Spatial Intelligence Automated Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) In-Orbit Assembly Perception-Guided High-Precision Manipulation for In-Orbit Assembly

Evolving Digital Twins

High-fidelity digital models that stay in sync with a changing environment, from dynamic SLAM to 4D reconstruction of manufacturing processes.

Related projects Fine-Detailed 3D Modeling for Monitoring Manufacturing Processes Modeling and Simulation of Disruption for Resilient Infrastructures ModalForcast
