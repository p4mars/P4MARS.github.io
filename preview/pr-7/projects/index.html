Transferable Spatial Intelligence

Robots that understand a 3D scene not only as geometry, but as objects, their functional parts, and what can be done with them, and that carry this understanding across environments, tasks and embodiments. We combine 3D scene graphs with vision-language foundation models to obtain open-vocabulary, task-driven perception that transfers to new settings without retraining. Outcomes so far include T-FunS3D (ICRA 2026) and a live open-vocabulary navigation and manipulation demo at Robots in Business 2026.

Research pillars Spatial & Agentic Intelligence Actionable World Models
Sponsored by Aerospace Engineering Robotics Institute

Luchtvaart in Transitie (LiT): Aviation in Transition

Luchtvaart in Transitie is a Dutch national programme that brings together government, knowledge institutes and industry to accelerate the transition to climate-neutral aviation by 2050. Within LiT, p4MARS contributes perception and AI for the production and maintenance of next-generation aircraft through two activities: fine-detailed 3D modeling for monitoring manufacturing processes, and automated non-destructive testing.

Modeling and Simulation of Disruption for Resilient Infrastructures

Critical infrastructure must keep working when parts of it fail. We build perception-driven digital models of infrastructure and simulate how disruptions emerge and propagate through them, so that operators can anticipate failures, stress-test response strategies, and plan inspection, repair and maintenance for more resilient systems.

Research pillars Spatial & Agentic Intelligence Evolving Digital Twins
Sponsored by Safety & Security Institute

Open-Source Interactive Education Materials for ML and Foundation Models

We develop open, interactive learning materials (notebooks, visual demos and hands-on exercises) that let students explore machine learning and foundation models by running and modifying them, rather than only reading about them. The materials are used in our courses and are freely available for others to reuse.
