About

Robust autonomy through perception

The p4MARS Lab is an interdisciplinary research group focused on Perception and AI for Manufacturing Automation, Robotics, and Space, working at the intersection of robotics, agentic AI, and cognitive systems. We target two classes of problems: automating high-variation tasks in complex and dynamic environments, such as inspection, repair and maintenance, and advanced manufacturing; and building the intelligence behind adaptive, resilient autonomous systems for extreme and unstructured environments, notably space.

Robust autonomy is achieved through advanced perception coupled with high-fidelity digital models that mirror the physical world, enabling embodied and physically grounded intelligence.

Spatial & Agentic Intelligence

Geometric and semantic scene understanding, 3D scene graphs, and open-vocabulary perception that let robots reason about where things are and what they can do with them.

Actionable World Models

Interaction-aware models of the physical world that couple perception with action, supporting active and distributed perception for embodied, physically grounded agents.

Evolving Digital Twins

High-fidelity digital models that stay in sync with a changing environment, from dynamic SLAM to 4D reconstruction of manufacturing processes.

Lab lead

Reza Sabzevari

Reza Sabzevari Associate Professor TU Delft | AE

Reza is an Associate Professor of Robotics and Perception. He leads the p4MARS Lab: an interdisciplinary research group focused on Perception and AI for Manufacturing Automation, Robotics, and Space.

Before joining TU Delft, Reza was a senior scientist at Bosch Research and the Bosch Centre for Artificial Intelligence (BCAI) in Germany, where he founded the x.Lab, a playground for experimenting robot perception algorithms. His research interests include geometric and semantic scene understanding, active perception, and distributed perception, with the application to advanced robotics automation and space domains.
